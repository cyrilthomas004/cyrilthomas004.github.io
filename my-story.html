Every career has a defining moment. Mine wasn't a single event-it was a gradual realization that helping people learn was what I genuinely enjoyed the most.

After completing my Master's in Communication, I began my professional journey in advertising. It was an exciting environment that strengthened my creativity, communication and problem-solving skills. More importantly, it taught me how thoughtful communication influences the way people think, understand and connect.

My path then led me into academia, where I spent more than five years as an Assistant Professor and Coordinator for Media courses. Teaching students, mentoring them, designing course content and creating meaningful learning experiences became the most rewarding part of my work. Looking back, this was where I unknowingly laid the foundation for a career in instructional design.

Looking for an opportunity to create learning at a larger scale, I transitioned into instructional design. Working on diverse learning solutions, collaborating with talented teams and solving business challenges confirmed what I had gradually realised over the years—this was the field I truly wanted to build my career in.

Today, I continue exploring new approaches, tools and technologies that make learning engaging, practical and meaningful. I believe great learning isn't about delivering information—it's about creating experiences that help people feel confident enough to take the next step.
